Companies (for payslips)

Each registered company you actually pay employees out of, with its own SARS payroll reference numbers. Add and save your companies here first, then link each department to one below in Employee departments — that's what decides which company's letterhead shows up on a given employee's payslip.

Employee departments

Managed here so everyone adding/editing an employee on the Payroll tab picks from the same list. Renaming a department here updates every employee currently assigned to it (active or inactive) to match. Deactivating one just hides it from the suggestions without relabelling anyone, and removing one from the list entirely leaves existing employees exactly as they are, since there's no new name to move them to. Link a department to a Company (above) to control which company's PAYE/UIF/SDL numbers appear on that department's payslips.

Expense category aliases

JHB (Zoho Books) and Veg/Pyroca (Sage) name some of the same real expense differently — e.g. "Salaries and Employee Wages" vs "Salaries & Wages" — which used to show up as two separate bars on the Dashboard's Combined expense breakdown. Map an alias to the canonical name you want it grouped under here; this only affects the Combined view — each entity's own Dashboard view always shows its exact wording from the source P&L, untouched.
